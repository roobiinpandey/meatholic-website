The Wagyu Craft

We believe great meat should be kept simple, cooked with care, and served the way it’s meant to be enjoyed.

“Wagyu does not need heavy seasoning. Salt is enough because the fat already carries most of the flavor.”

— Meatholic Philosophy

“Wagyu needs gentle handling. Too much pressure or rough handling can damage its texture.”

— Meatholic Philosophy
